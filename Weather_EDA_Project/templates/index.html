PHYSICAL LAWS
🚨
Zero pressure is a strong data quality flag

1,288 rows contain 0.0 mb. This is implausible as a surface pressure reading, but its meaning (missing-value sentinel, logging issue, or other encoding) must be confirmed from the source documentation.

⚠️
Zero humidity deserves review

22 rows show 0.00 humidity. This may reflect rounding, a lower-bound convention, or a sensor/data issue; the value alone does not identify the cause.

❌
“Loud Cover” is constant zero in this file

The column has no variation and cannot distinguish records in this dataset. Its intended meaning is unclear from the values alone.

ℹ️
Visibility has a maximum of about 16.1 km

A repeated upper bound can indicate a measurement ceiling, but confirm the instrument and source convention before treating these observations as censored.
